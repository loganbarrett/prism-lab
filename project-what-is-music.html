Music cognition · In development

What Is Music?

How do listeners decide what counts as music? Rate clips from music, speech, poetry, and everyday sounds.

← All projects

Participants rate short clips drawn from twelve categories, from instrumental music to poetry and environmental sounds, on a seven-point scale. The aim is to map the boundaries of the category 'music' across listeners.

What is measured

  • Seven-point musicality ratings
  • Category-level rating profiles
  • Individual differences in category boundaries
