Join the Lab

We welcome curious people from many backgrounds.

PRISM Lab brings together people interested in music, voice, hearing, brains, and models.

Who might fit

  • Students and trainees in psychology, neuroscience, music, speech and hearing sciences, computer science, or related fields
  • Collaborators with complementary expertise
  • Community members interested in volunteering as research participants

How to get in touch

Email the lab with a short note about your interests and background. Open positions will be listed here when available.
