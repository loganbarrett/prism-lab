Online Consent Form: Speech or Song?

You are invited to take part in a research study run by the Auditory Perception & Action Lab, Department of Research, Niagara Falls Memorial Medical Center. The person in charge of the study is Logan Barrett. Please read this page before you decide.

Why we are doing this study

Some sounds sit between speech and song: poetry, rap, chanting, a talkative singer. We want to understand where people draw the line between speech and song, and how that line differs between people.

What you will do

  • First you will answer a few optional background questions (see below). You can skip any of them.
  • You will hear short phrases, about 1 to 3 seconds each, taken from real recordings of people reading aloud, talking, reading poetry, rapping and singing, in English and other languages.
  • After each phrase you swipe left if it sounds like speech to you, or right if it sounds like song. There are no right or wrong answers.
  • At the end you will see how often you called each kind of recording a song.

It takes about 8 to 10 minutes. Headphones are recommended. You must be 18 years old or older to take part in the main study. If you are younger, there is a separate form for young people and their parent or guardian; the game page will show it when you enter your age.

Possible risks and benefits

There is no risk beyond that of everyday life. Some recordings come from public archives and may include strong language or mature themes in a language you may or may not understand. You can stop at any time. The benefit to you is seeing how your answers compare across kinds of recording. There is no payment. The study may help researchers understand how people tell speech from song.

What information we collect

  • Which phrases you heard and whether you called each one speech or song.
  • How long you took to answer after each phrase ended, and how often you replayed it.
  • Your answers to the optional background questions: age, gender, country, first language, years of music training, how often you make music, hours of listening per day, what you are listening on, and whether you have hearing difficulty.
  • An anonymous participant ID that the page makes up, the date and time, and the version of this consent form.

We do not ask for your name or email address, and we do not use your microphone or record any sound from you. Like most websites, the server that hosts this page may keep ordinary technical logs, such as your IP address. These logs are not part of the research data. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps such logs for security; the researchers do not see them.

How your information will be used

Your answers will be combined with those of other people and analysed to study how people tell speech from song. Results may appear in scientific papers, presentations and teaching materials, always as group results or as anonymous data. The anonymous data may be shared in an open research repository.

Where your information is kept

Your anonymous data file is stored in a password-protected folder belonging to the lab on Google Drive and on a password-protected computer and is kept for 10 years. Because the data are anonymous, nobody can tell from the file who you are.

Taking part is voluntary

You do not have to take part. If you do not agree, you can still play the game on this page: no questions are asked and no data are sent. If you agree, you may stop at any time by closing the page. The page shows your anonymous participant ID at the end. If you would like your data removed, send that ID to the contact below. Without the ID we cannot find your file.

Questions

If you have questions about this study, contact Logan Barrett (logan.barrett@nfmmc.org). If you have questions about your rights as a research participant, contact the Niagara Falls Memorial Medical Center IRB office at irb@nfmmc.org. The IRB study number is IRB20261103.

Agreeing

If you tick the box and press I agree on the game page, you are confirming that you are at least 18 years old, that you have read this form, and that you agree to take part. If you do not wish to take part, choose Just play, don't share my data, or close the page.
