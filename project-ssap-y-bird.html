SSAP-y Bird

A game-based version of the Seattle Singing Accuracy Protocol that measures how accurately people sing and discriminate pitch.

← All projects

SSAP-y Bird turns the Seattle Singing Accuracy Protocol into a browser game. Participants sing back notes and short sequences, sing a familiar song, and complete an adaptive pitch-discrimination task. Pitch is tracked in the browser with the YIN algorithm, and scoring follows the original SSAP procedures. Recordings and trial data are saved only with consent.

What is measured

  • Single-note imitation accuracy (cents)
  • Four-note sequence imitation
  • Familiar-song singing accuracy
  • Pitch-discrimination threshold
