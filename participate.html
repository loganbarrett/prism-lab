What to expect

Each study starts with a consent form and a few optional background questions. Most use headphones or speakers; singing studies also use your microphone.

Your rights

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time without penalty. Consent forms describe what data are collected and how they are stored.

Questions

Contact the lab at our contact page. Research-participant questions go to the institutional review board listed on each consent form.
