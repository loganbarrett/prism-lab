About PRISM Lab

Perception, Representation, Identity, and Sound in Music

PRISM Lab studies the cognitive, neural, computational, perceptual, and social mechanisms underlying music cognition, singing, vocal production, auditory perception, and identity.

Why a prism?

A prism takes one beam of light and separates it into the components that were always inside it. We use the same idea for sound. A single voice or melody carries pitch, timing, timbre, expectation, and identity at once. Our work separates these strands, studies each with the right method, and then puts them back together.

How we work

  • Interdisciplinary. Behavioral science, neuroimaging, acoustics, and machine learning inform one another.
  • Open and transparent. We aim to share materials, code, and preregistrations where participants' privacy and licensing allow.
  • Inclusive. We aim to build tasks and measures that are valid for diverse voices, identities, and musical backgrounds.
  • Accessible online. Many of our studies run in a web browser, so people can take part from anywhere.
